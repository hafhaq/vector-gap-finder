    How to Read the Map & Trust the Gaps

    VoidLens turns a sea of existing products, papers, and discussions into a map of ideas—then uses geometry to point at the empty courtyards surrounded by crowded neighborhoods. This guide explains every number, every button, and how to judge whether a suggested gap is genuinely real.

    1. The Big Idea: A Neighborhood Map of Concepts

    Imagine laying out every industrial PLC product, patent, or research paper on a giant table so that ideas that do similar jobs sit close together and ideas that do different jobs sit far apart.

    • Colored dots are existing things (products, papers, open-source projects, or articles collected from the web).
    • Glowing blue/teal clouds show crowdedness (lots of competing ideas piled on top of each other).
    • Amber diamonds (#1, #2, …) are detected gaps—empty spots sitting between two or more busy neighborhoods where nobody has built a bridge yet.
    Why "far away" alone is NOT a good gap: "Banana smoothies" is very far from Industrial PLCs, so its empty space is huge—but it's useless. VoidLens specifically hunts for the Goldilocks zone: spots that are empty right now, yet surrounded on multiple sides by real industry capabilities.

    2. How Can I Trust the "Gravity" of Nodes & Whether a Gap Is Valid?

    This is the most important question you can ask. A chart with glowing dots can easily look scientific even when it's misleading. Here is how VoidLens earns (and tests) your trust—and where you should stay skeptical:

    A. What creates the "gravity" pulling nodes together?

    Every document is turned into a list of numbers (a vector) representing its meaning:

    • LSA-SVD (default, instant): Reads shared technical words, two-word phrases (like "digital twin" or "zero trust"), and word roots. Great for fast exploration, but it can miss synonyms that share no spelling (like "latency" vs. "delay").
    • Neural 384-D (click Neural 384-D in the top bar): Runs a real AI language model (MiniLM-L6-v2) directly inside your browser. It understands meaning and synonyms, not just spelling. For high-stakes decisions, always turn on Neural 384-D.

    B. Doesn't squishing ideas onto a flat 2D screen distort distances?

    Yes—just like flattening the round Earth onto a flat map stretches Greenland! That is why VoidLens does two crucial things:

    1. The gap math runs in full high-dimensional space, NOT on the flat 2D drawing. Every cos number, density reading, and topological hole is measured in the full 32-D or 384-D space before anything is projected onto your screen.
    2. We display an Honesty Meter right on the chart:
      • Trust T(k) (e.g., 84%): Tells you how often two dots that look like neighbors on screen are actually neighbors in full meaning-space. Above 80% is solid; below 65% means rely on the right-hand numbers more than visual proximity.
      • Stress-1 (e.g., 0.24): Measures overall distance stretching (lower is better; under 0.25 is good).

    C. Four Proof Checks That Separate Real Gaps from Glitches

    Before you bet time or money on any #1 gap, inspect its four scorecard meters and run this 4-step sanity check:

    1. Check the Corpus (Garbage In = Fake Gaps)

    If your search only pulled 18 papers and missed a major vendor category, VoidLens will call that missing vendor a "gap." Click Corpus (N) or Sources to verify the major players are actually in the dataset.

    2. Verify the Surrounding Anchors

    Look at Surrounding Frontier Offerings in the right panel. Do those 3–4 existing items genuinely make sense together? If they look unrelated, the gap may be a corpus artifact.

    3. Look for H₁ Cavity & Bridge Entropy

    A gap marked H1 CAVITY with high Bridge Entropy (> 60%) means real existing products form a closed ring around an empty center bridging 2–3 distinct domains—a much stronger signal than an isolated edge outlier.

    4. Upgrade to Neural 384-D & Re-Check

    If a gap survives when switching from LSA-SVD to Neural 384-D, it's a real semantic void—not just a vocabulary coincidence.

    Important Reality Check: Math can prove that "nobody has combined X and Y in this corpus." Math cannot prove whether combining X and Y is physically possible, profitable, or legal—sometimes a space is empty because it's a bad idea or blocked by physics/regulation. VoidLens hands you high-signal hypotheses; your domain judgment decides which ones are gold.

    3. Decoding Every Number, cos Value, and Percentage

    Every badge in VoidLens has a plain-English meaning:

    Label you see Plain-English meaning How to read the number
    cos 0.72 Cosine Similarity — how aligned two ideas are in meaning (from −1 to +1). 0.80 – 1.00: Nearly the same thing
    0.55 – 0.79: Close cousins / same category
    0.30 – 0.54: Related neighbors
    < 0.25: Very different concepts
    White-Space Index: 84% Overall opportunity score combining emptiness, cross-cluster bridging, relevance, and topological proof. 75%+ strong candidate; 50–74% moderate; < 45% either crowded or off-topic.
    Void Sparsity & d_min How empty the area is around this gap. d_min is the distance to the closest existing product (1 − cos). Higher % = fewer competitors nearby. d_min > 0.45 means no existing item in the corpus is close.
    Bridge Entropy Does this gap connect different fields (e.g., OT Security + Motion Control) rather than sitting inside one silo? 70%+ = true cross-disciplinary bridge between 2–4 clusters. 0% = surrounded by only one cluster.
    Domain Hull Support & vMF ρ Sanity check proving the gap actually belongs to your topic rather than drifting into random unrelated space. 60%+ = firmly inside your industry's footprint. Very low % = likely off-topic noise.
    H₁ Cavity Alignment Whether the gap sits inside a mathematically verified "donut hole" (a closed loop of existing ideas). 50%+ = backed by a real loop of surrounding ideas. continuous = open valley between clusters.
    nov 74% (on decoded concepts) Novelty — how rarely that suggested capability appears anywhere in your current corpus. High cos + high nov % = a capability that fits the gap AND isn't already saturated in the market.
    Burt 0.42 (Cluster list) How "boxed in" a cluster is (sociologist Ronald Burt's Structural Holes score). Lower Burt = that cluster touches many different worlds (good broker). Higher Burt = isolated silo.

    Technical Specification · For Data Scientists, ML Engineers & Applied Topologists

    Mathematical Architecture, Fidelity Bounds & Epistemic Limits

    VoidLens formulates niche discovery as interior density-valley and 1-dimensional topological cavity detection on the unit hypersphere Sd−1, paired with orthogonal subspace concept inversion. Below are the exact operators, scoring equations, distortion diagnostics, and known failure modes.

    1. Hyperspherical Embedding Spaces (Sd−1)

    All corpus items {x₁, …, xₙ} and cross-domain lexicon primitives {l₁, …, lₘ} are embedded and L₂-normalized onto the unit hypersphere S^(d−1), where cosine similarity s(u, v) = uᵀv ∈ [−1, 1], cosine distance d_cos(u, v) = 1 − uᵀv ∈ [0, 2], and intrinsic geodesic distance d_g(u, v) = arccos(uᵀv).

    Engine A: Dual-Gram Truncated SVD (LSA-SVD, Instant)

    • Feature Extraction: Sublinear TF 1 + ln(tf) weighted by smoothed corpus+lexicon IDF over stemmed unigrams (weight 1.0), word bigrams (weight 0.85), and padded character 4-grams (weight 0.22) for technical morphology invariance.
    • Exact Decomposition: Rather than solving a V × V vocabulary eigensystem, VoidLens forms the N × N Dual Gram Matrix G = X Xᵀ and solves G U = U Λ via exact Jacobi symmetric rotations (residual ‖Av − λv‖ < 10⁻⁷).
    • Caron Anisotropy Mitigation: Latent coordinates apply mild singular power scaling Z_i[r] = U_r[i] · σ_r^0.85 (Caron 2001), reducing hubness on S^(k−1) before unit renormalization.
    • Exact Out-of-Sample Folding-In: Any lexicon term or vector-algebra query q projects identically via z(q)[r] = σ_r^(−0.15) Σᵢ (q · xᵢ) U_r[i].

    Engine B: Neural 384-D Transformer (Xenova/all-MiniLM-L6-v2)

    • Runs quantized ONNX (q8) inside a dedicated Web Worker via WebGPU (automatic WASM fallback) with mean pooling and L₂ normalization into S^383.
    • Both corpus items and the full lexicon are embedded in the same pretrained semantic geometry, capturing deep paraphrase/synonym equivalence beyond lexical overlap.

    2. Trusting "Node Gravity" & Inter-Node Space

    A core pitfall in visual analytics is mistaking 2D layout artifacts for real geometric structure. VoidLens decouples high-dimensional measurement from 2D visualization:

    • All Core Computations Occur in Full Rd: Spherical KDE, Vietoris–Rips H₀/H₁ persistence, spherical k-Means++, Burt constraint, nearest-neighbor distances, and orthogonal concept decoding are computed strictly from the full d-dimensional vectors—never from 2D pixel coordinates (the lone 2D-seeded generator, Delaunay/Voronoi circumcenters, is RBF-lifted back onto S^(d−1) and scored in full R^d).
    • Projection Options:
      • UMAP Manifold: Initializes from Exact PCA (preserving deterministic global orientation), constructs a symmetric fuzzy simplicial k-NN graph Pᵢⱼ = pᵢ|ⱼ + pⱼ|ᵢ − pᵢ|ⱼpⱼ|ᵢ with binary-searched local bandwidth σᵢ matching log₂(k), and optimizes attractive/repulsive manifold forces with distance-weighted repulsion to preserve inter-cluster cavities.
      • Exact PCA: Dual-Gram centered PCA projecting onto exact primal axes PC₁, PC₂, reporting exact PC₁ + PC₂ variance explained and the top-12 eigenvalue scree spectrum.
    • Real-Time Distortion Diagnostics (Top-Left Stage Badges):
      • Venna & Kaski (2001) Trustworthiness T(k) ∈ [0, 1]: Penalizes "false neighbors" that appear close in 2D but are distant in R^d:
        T(k) = 1 − [2 / (N k (2N − 3k − 1))] Σᵢ Σ_{j ∈ U_k(i)} (r(i, j) − k)
        Interpretation: T(k) ≥ 0.85 indicates high local faithfulness; < 0.70 warns that 2D neighborhoods contain fold-over illusions.
      • Scale-Invariant Kruskal Stress-1:
        Stress₁ = sqrt( Σ (d_H(i,j) − α·d_L(i,j))² / Σ d_H(i,j)² )
        Interpretation: < 0.15 excellent global metric preservation; 0.15–0.28 fair; > 0.30 rely on the k-NN graph overlay and Rd cosine tables rather than raw 2D Euclidean spans.

    3. Four Independent Candidate Generators & Validity Criteria

    To avoid single-heuristic bias, VoidLens synthesizes candidate gap vectors g ∈ S^(d−1) via four complementary geometric/topological mechanisms, then applies spherical + 2D Non-Maximum Suppression (d_cos < 0.14 or d_2D < 0.18):

    1. Vietoris–Rips Persistent Homology (h1-cavity): Builds the filtered 2-skeleton on pairwise cosine distances Dᵢⱼ = 1 − vᵢᵀvⱼ. H₀ is computed via Kruskal Union-Find; H₁ is computed via exact left-to-right column reduction of the boundary matrix ∂₂ over ℤ₂ (Edelsbrunner–Letscher–Zomorodian 2002). Each non-trivial birth-death pair (b, d) yields persistence p = d − b, and the reduced killer column directly returns the exact bounding 1-cycle of corpus vertices. Its intrinsic Karcher/Fréchet mean on S^(d−1) defines the cavity center.
    2. von Mises–Fisher (vMF) KDE & Riemannian Descent (riemannian-min): Models spherical density via shifted vMF kernel estimation:
      f̂_κ(x) = (1/N) Σᵢ exp(κ (xᵀvᵢ − 1)) ∈ (0, 1]
      Candidate seeds descend the regularized log-density energy E(x) = ln(f̂_κ(x) + ε) + λ_hull (1 − xᵀμ_domain)² using Riemannian tangent-space gradients:
      ∇_S E(x) = ∇E(x) − (xᵀ ∇E(x)) x
      with geodesic retraction onto S^(d−1). The λ_hull = 2.6 restoring force guarantees valleys stay inside the domain's semantic convex support instead of escaping to orthogonal nonsense.
    3. Inter-Cluster Geodesic Bridges (geodesic-bridge): Partitions S^(d−1) via multi-restart Spherical k-Means++ (maximizing total cosine cohesion) and computes great-circle SLERP midpoints slerp(μₐ, μ_b, 0.5) between all distinct cluster pairs, plus Burt's Structural-Hole Constraint Cᵢ = Σⱼ (pᵢⱼ + Σ_q pᵢ_q p_qj)² on the cluster affinity graph.
    4. Bowyer–Watson Delaunay & α-Shape Voids (voronoi-void): Computes exact 2D Delaunay triangulation, rejects hull-boundary sliver triangles outside the α-radius quantile, lifts top interior Voronoi circumcenters back to S^(d−1) via Gaussian RBF weighted Fréchet means, and blends with the spherical mean of the bounding triangle vertices.

    4. Composite White-Space Index & Metric Definitions

    Every auto-discovered gap, manual Void Probe (⊕), and Vector Algebra target (∑) is evaluated identically on S^(d−1):

    Opportunity(g) = Gate(Coherence) × [ w₁·Sparsity + w₂·BridgeEntropy + w₃·Coherence + w₄·TopoPersistence ] / Σw
    Gate(Coherence) = 0.40 + 0.60 · Coherence^0.6
    Metric Exact Formula / Operational Definition Purpose
    Sparsity (w₁ = 0.38) 0.58 · clip(d_min / (1.15 · d̄_pair)) + 0.42 · (1 − ρ_norm) where d_min = minᵢ(1 − gᵀvᵢ) and ρ_norm normalizes f̂_κ(g) to corpus range. Combines hard empty-ball radius with smooth spherical density scarcity.
    Bridge Entropy (w₂ = 0.30) Normalized Shannon entropy H = −Σ_c p_c ln(p_c) / ln(min(4, K)) over softmax-weighted cluster shares of the top-10 neighbors (β = 6.5). Quantifies Burt-style structural hole bridging across distinct clusters.
    Domain Coherence (w₃ = 0.18) clip( [0.5·max(0, gᵀμ_dom) + 0.5·mean_top5(gᵀvᵢ)] / max(0.28, 1 − 0.55·d̄_pair) ). Also drives the multiplicative Gate. Prevents out-of-distribution outer-space vectors from achieving high scores.
    H₁ Persistence (w₄ = 0.14) max_c [ (pers_c / pers_max) · max(0, gᵀbary_c)³ ] across the top-12 persistent H₁ cycles. Rewards gaps aligned with verified topological cavities in full R^d.
    Vector Algebra (∑) g = normalize( embed(A) + embed(B) − 0.65 · embed(C) ), projected via exact PCA or Nadaraya–Watson geodesic kernel barycenters (k = 7). Hypothesis-driven probing of compositional concepts in the manifold.

    5. The Inverse Problem: Decoding a Gap Vector Without an LLM

    A point g ∈ S^(d−1) has no intrinsic text label. To decode what is missing at g rather than merely echoing its existing neighbors, VoidLens performs Modified Gram–Schmidt Orthogonal Residual Projection:

    B = orthonormalize( {v_{i₁}, v_{i₂}, v_{i₃}, v_{i₄}} ) // top-4 nearest existing offerings
    g_⊥ = normalize( g − Σ_{q ∈ B} (gᵀq) q ) // novel orthogonal residual
    Score(l_m) = 0.52·max(0, gᵀl_m) + 0.28·max(0, g_⊥ᵀl_m) + 0.20·novelty_m·((gᵀl_m + 1)/2)
    novelty_m = 1 − maxᵢ( l_mᵀvᵢ ) // corpus saturation penalty

    This penalizes capabilities already saturated in the corpus and elevates cross-domain primitives aligned specifically with the unspanned directions of the void.

    6. Epistemic Limits & Rigorous Validation Checklist

    Every statistical discovery tool has well-defined boundaries. Keep these five failure modes explicit when interpreting outputs:

    • Corpus Selection Bias (Closed-World Assumption): A gap is strictly relative to the ingested corpus {x₁, …, xₙ}. Proprietary stealth products, paywalled patents, or topics underrepresented on OpenAlex/Wikipedia/HN/GitHub can produce sampling voids rather than true market voids. Always merge domain-specific specs via Corpus (N) before drawing strategic conclusions.
    • Closed Lexicon Ceiling in Inversion: Orthogonal Concept Inversion ranks primitives from VoidLens's curated ~70-concept cross-domain lexicon. If the true missing capability lies outside this dictionary, treat the decoded terms as directional analogies and use Copy Brief to synthesize further with an external LLM or domain expert.
    • Concentration of Measure in High Dimensions: In S^383 (MiniLM), pairwise cosine distances concentrate tightly around the mean; that is why VoidLens normalizes Sparsity against the corpus's empirical d̄_pair and vMF density range, and lets you tune concentration κ.
    • Lexical vs. Semantic Gaps: In LSA-SVD mode, two synonyms without shared character 4-grams can look artificially distant. Always verify high-priority voids after toggling Neural 384-D.
    • Structural Hole ≠ Commercial Viability: Persistent homology proves topological emptiness in representation space—it does not distinguish between an untapped breakthrough and an infeasible, uneconomic, or regulatorily barred combination.
